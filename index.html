Draft push helper

A private, single-operator tool that sends image files to the operator's own TikTok inbox as drafts. It serves no users other than its operator.

Terms of service

This tool is operated by its owner for the owner's own TikTok accounts only. No account other than the operator's is connected, and no third party may use it.

Privacy policy

The tool stores the operator's own TikTok access tokens on the operator's computer and nothing else. Images are hosted here only for the minutes TikTok needs to fetch them, then removed. No analytics, no cookies, no data about anyone else.
